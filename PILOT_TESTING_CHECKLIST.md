# Kazi Pilot — Tester Checklist

Thank you for helping us test Kazi, a simple business notebook for shops and small
businesses. This is a **test version**, not the final one. Please use **pretend (test)
information only** — do not enter real customer names, real money figures, or anything
private.

You do not need to be good with phones or computers. Just try the steps below and tell us
what happened using the feedback form.

**What you need**

- A phone with the Kazi test app installed.
- Internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
- About 15–20 minutes.

**Before you start**

- Ask the Kazi team for a test account, or create your own with a pretend email.
- It is fine to make mistakes — this is what testing is for.

---

## Step 1 — Install and open Kazi

1. Install the Kazi app on your phone, then tap its icon to open it.
2. Wait for the first screen to appear.

- [ ] The app opened without crashing or freezing.
- [ ] The first screen was easy to understand.

Notes: ______________________________________________

## Step 2 — Create an account and sign in again

1. Create an account with a name, a **pretend** email and a password.
2. Fill in your business name if the app asks.
3. Close the app completely, open it again, and check you are still signed in.
4. Sign out (in the **More** tab) and sign back in with the same email and password.

- [ ] I could create an account.
- [ ] I stayed signed in after reopening the app.
- [ ] I could sign out and sign back in.

Notes: ______________________________________________

## Step 3 — Record a test transaction

1. Go to the **Home** tab and tap **Record Sale** (or **Record Expense**).
2. Type a small pretend amount, for example `1000`, keep today's date, and save.

- [ ] The transaction saved with no error.
- [ ] A short confirmation message appeared.

Notes: ______________________________________________

## Step 4 — Find a previous transaction

1. Open the **History** tab.
2. Look for the transaction you just saved.
3. Try the period buttons (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Older**).

- [ ] I could find the transaction I saved.
- [ ] The period buttons changed what was shown.

Notes: ______________________________________________

## Step 5 — Check the numbers are correct

1. Compare the totals shown on the **Home** tab with what you recorded.
2. If you recorded a sale of `1000`, "Money received today" should include `1000 FCFA`.

- [ ] The amount shown matched what I entered.
- [ ] The totals added up correctly.

Notes: ______________________________________________

## Step 6 — Close and reopen the app

1. Close the app completely (from the phone's app switcher).
2. Open it again after a minute.
3. Check your transaction and totals are still there.
4. If you have a second phone or turn off data and back on, note whether anything changes.

- [ ] My transaction was still there after reopening.
- [ ] The totals were still correct after reopening.

Notes: ______________________________________________

## Step 7 — Report anything that went wrong

Tell us about every problem, however small.

1. Did the app close by itself, freeze, or show an error?
2. Did any screen or word confuse you?
3. Was anything missing that you expected to find?
4. Could you send us feedback? (Open **More → Send Feedback** in the app.)

- [ ] I reported at least one thing (or confirmed everything worked).

Notes: ______________________________________________

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The app says it cannot connect:** check your internet, then try again.
- **Something looks stuck:** close the app and open it again.
- **You forgot your password:** use "Forgot password", and if it does not work, tell the
  Kazi team (password reset by email is not switched on yet in the test version).
- **Anything else:** write it down and send it to the Kazi team. A photo of the screen
  helps a lot.

Please return this checklist together with your completed feedback form.
